Training Menu

FTP 245 W  ·  Monday & Friday rest

Tuesday

Hard cycling · choose one

Rønnestad 30/15 45 min

Default VO₂ session
  1. 10 minProgressive warm-up; include 2 × 20 sec fast efforts.
  2. 3 × 11Repeat 30 sec at 300–320 W, then 15 sec at 130–155 W. Eleven repetitions per set.
  3. 3 minEasy pedaling between sets (twice).
  4. 4:15Easy cooldown.

Keep hard efforts consistent. If ERG transitions lag, use resistance mode.

VO₂ 4 × 4 45 min

Longer VO₂ efforts
  1. 12 minProgressive warm-up, including 2 × 20 sec brisk efforts.
  2. 4 × 44 min at 270–285 W.
  3. 3 minEasy pedaling between intervals (three times).
  4. 8 minCooldown.
Zwift race 45–50 min

Race instead of intervals
  1. 12 minWarm up progressively, with 3 × 20 sec hard accelerations.
  2. 25–30Race a rolling or punchy course. Push for position, follow surges, then recover while staying in the group.
  3. 8 minEasy cooldown.

Pick a race expected to last 25–30 minutes so the whole session fits.

Wednesday

Strength · choose one

Full-body strength 56 min

Default strength day
  1. 0–5Dynamic hip / ankle prep; squat, hinge, and lunge patterns.
  2. 5–8Vertical jumps 3 × 3, crisp and explosive.
  3. 8–11Squat ramp: bar × 8 → 135 lb × 5 → 165 lb × 3.
  4. 11–22Back squat 3 × 5 @ 205 lb; rest 2.5–3 min.
  5. 22–31Trap-bar deadlift 2 × 6 @ 205 lb + pull-ups 2 × 5–8.
  6. 31–41Bulgarian split squat 2 × 6/leg + barbell overhead press 2 × 6–8 @ 75 lb, RPE ≈ 8.
  7. 41–49Copenhagen plank 2 × 20–30 sec/side + suitcase carry 2 × 30–45 sec/side.
  8. 49–56Single-leg calf raise 2 × 10–15 + push-ups 2 sets, ~2 reps in reserve.

Use these as working loads, not mandatory targets. Leave 1–3 reps in reserve on heavy leg lifts.

Short strength 25 min

Lower-fatigue alternative
  1. 5 minDynamic warm-up and squat / hinge ramp-up.
  2. 8 minBack squat or trap-bar deadlift 2 × 3, moderate-heavy, no grinding.
  3. 5 minBulgarian split squat 1 × 5/leg.
  4. 7 minCopenhagen plank 2 × 20 sec/side + suitcase carry 1 × 30 sec/side.

Skip strength entirely if recovery is poor.

Thursday

Supporting ride · choose one

Tempo 45 min

Default steady ride
  1. 7 minEasy progressive warm-up.
  2. 2 × 15195–210 W at comfortable cadence.
  3. 4 minEasy pedaling between intervals.
  4. 4 minCooldown.
Surge + settle 45 min

Practice repeated accelerations
  1. 8 minWarm-up.
  2. 3 × 9Each 9-minute block: 20 sec at 300–330 W then 2:40 at 185–205 W; repeat that pattern three times. No coasting after the surge.
  3. 3 minEasy pedaling between blocks (twice).
  4. 4 minCooldown.

This is another demanding workout; use it when recovered from Tuesday and before a controlled Saturday.

Easy endurance 45 min

Keep Thursday light
  1. 5 minEasy warm-up.
  2. 35 minRide steadily at 150–175 W.
  3. 5 minEasy cooldown.
Heat ride 45 min

Short heat exposure
  1. 5 minEasy warm-up with normal fan cooling.
  2. 35 minEasy 120–155 W in a genuinely warm room with some airflow. Reduce power as needed.
  3. 5 minCool down with normal fan cooling.

Heat is the stressor, not watts. Don't deliberately dehydrate.

Saturday

Outdoor ride · choose one

Gravel endurance 2:30

Default long ride
  1. 15 minStart easy: 135–155 W.
  2. 2:05On rideable gravel, aim for 150–180 W. Let climbs, coasting, and rough terrain vary the power naturally. Include 20 minutes of rougher gravel in the second half; focus on braking, line choice, and smooth reacceleration.
  3. 10 minEasy ride home.

Fuel consistently; a familiar starting target is 60–80 g carbohydrate per hour, adjusted to gut tolerance.

Gravel surges 3:00

Harder long ride
  1. 15 minEasy warm-up at 135–155 W.
  2. 90 minSteady gravel at 150–180 W on rideable terrain. Ride the climbs naturally.
  3. 3 × 15Hold 185–205 W between surges. At minutes 3, 8 and 13 of each block, accelerate for 20 sec at 300–330 W; settle immediately back to 185–205 W.
  4. 5 minEasy pedaling between blocks (twice).
  5. 20 minEasy finish.

Do the hard blocks on safe, relatively smooth terrain. Fuel throughout.

Easy gravel 1:30

Short or recovery ride
  1. 15 minEasy start.
  2. 65 minComfortable ride at 145–165 W on rideable terrain; no planned surges.
  3. 10 minEasy finish.

Sunday

Endurance, heat, or rest

Second-day endurance 75 min

Ride on Saturday's legs
  1. 10 minEasy warm-up.
  2. 55 minHold 150–175 W. Maintain a smooth cadence even if legs feel heavy.
  3. 10 minEasy cooldown.

Keep this controlled, not a second race.

Heat endurance 60 min

Longer heat exposure
  1. 5 minEasy with normal cooling.
  2. 50 minEasy 120–155 W in sustained warmth, with some airflow. Adjust power down if heat strain rises.
  3. 5 minEasy cooldown with normal cooling.

Hydrate normally; stop for dizziness, nausea or unusual weakness. Don't try to force dehydration.

Rest Off

After a demanding Saturday

No ride. An easy walk is fine.

Power ranges are based on FTP 245 W. Outdoors, prioritize control and safe handling over exact numbers. For heat riding, begin conservatively and avoid sessions when sick, dehydrated, or excessively fatigued.
