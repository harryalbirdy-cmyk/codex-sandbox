Selection rule: Tuesday is your one hard weekday (prefer Rønnestad or one Zwift race). Thursday is normally tempo/aerobic; choose repeated surges only when Tuesday was controlled and Saturday is easy. On a hard Saturday, Sunday stays easy/off. No three hard days.

Heat: Intro/45-min options are modest doses; meaningful acclimation takes repeated longer exposures. Use heat in place of easy work, not Tuesday VO₂ or normal Wednesday strength. Some airflow; hydrate; stop for dizziness, nausea, confusion or weakness. Long rides: practise ~60–75 g carbohydrate/hour initially and progress only as tolerated.

Tuesday

One hard day · ~45 min · Rønnestad or Zwift race
Preferred · VO₂

Rønnestad 30/15 · 45 min

Do this if: Default structured VO₂ workout; progress only if repeatable.

10 min progressive WU (include 2×20 sec primers) → 3×11 (30 sec ~300–320 W / 15 sec ~130–155 W) → 3 min easy between sets → 4:15 CD. 45:00 total. For classic 3×13, allow ~55 min with a 15-min WU. Choose repeatable work power, not all-out sprints; adjust after set 1. Resistance mode if ERG transitions are slow.

Workout

VO₂ 4×4 · 45 min

Do this if: Use longer sustained efforts instead of 30/15s.

12 min progressive WU → 4×4 min 270–285 W → 3 min easy between → 8 min CD. 45:00 total. Adjust for sustained hard but controlled breathing; do not force a watt target after repeated failures.

Workout

Over/Under · 45 min

Do this if: Target riding after surges, rather than VO₂ development.

8 min WU → 3×9 min (repeat 2 min 215–225 W + 1 min 260–275 W three times) → 3 min easy between → 4 min CD. 45:00 total.

Zwift race

Zwift Race · Punchy · 42–47 min

Do this if: Need spontaneous attacks and pack accelerations.

12 min progressive WU with 3×20 sec fast → 22–27 min rolling/crit race with repeated accelerations → 8 min CD. Pick by expected duration; stop at 50 min total.

Zwift race

Zwift Race · Hilly · 43–48 min

Do this if: Want longer, less predictable supra-threshold efforts.

10–12 min progressive WU → 25–30 min race featuring repeated 1–5 min climbs → 6 min CD. Choose a race that fits the total time.

Wednesday

Strength default · shorter only when needed
Workout

Full Strength · ~56 min

Do this if: Normal strength week; soreness stays manageable.

5 min dynamic WU → jumps 3×3 → squat ramp-up → back squat 3×5 @ 205 lb → trap-bar deadlift 2×6 @ 205 lb + pull-ups 2×5–8 → Bulgarian split squat 2×6/leg + OHP 2×6–8 @ 75 lb → Copenhagen plank 2×20–30 sec/side + suitcase carry 2×30–45 sec/side → single-leg calf raise 2×10–15 + push-ups 2 sets. Keep 1–3 reps in reserve; scale squat/deadlift loads if Tuesday was brutal.

Workout

Strength Microdose · 15–20 min

Do this if: Tuesday was hard or a demanding gravel weekend is coming.

3 min WU → squat or deadlift 2×3 at ~75–85% usual working load → split squat 1×5/leg → 3–5 min trunk stability. No grinders.

Workout

Recovery Spin · 30 min

Do this if: Legs tight; movement helps.

30 min 135–155 W.

Heat

Heat Ride · 45 min

Do this if: Strength intentionally skipped; seeking an easy heat exposure.

5 min easy with normal cooling → 35 min 120–155 W in a genuinely warm room with some airflow → 5 min cooled easy. This is a short heat exposure, not a full acclimation dose.

Workout

Off

Do this if: Fatigued or recovering poorly.

Full rest.

Thursday

Supporting aerobic / surges / heat · ~45 min
Workout

Tempo · 45 min

Do this if: Want steady aerobic work without another maximal day.

7 min WU → 2×15 min 195–205 W → 4 min easy between → 4 min CD. 45:00 total.

Workout

Repeated Surge + Settle · 45 min

Do this if: Tuesday was not maximal and Saturday is not a hard simulation.

8 min WU → 3×9 min at 185–205 W, inserting 20 sec at 300–330 W at minutes 0, 3 and 6 of each block; immediately return to steady power → 3 min easy between blocks → 4 min CD. 45:00 total.

Workout

Cadence / Torque Tempo · 45 min

Do this if: Gravel climbing/torque is a weakness; legs feel fresh.

8 min WU → 3×8 min 190–205 W at 65–75 rpm with smooth seated force → 4 min easy between → 5 min CD. 45:00 total. Avoid if knees or fit are irritated.

Workout

Aerobic · 45 min

Do this if: Tuesday, Wednesday strength, or Saturday is demanding.

5 min gentle → 35 min 150–175 W steady → 5 min easy. 45:00 total.

Heat

Heat Endurance · 45 min

Do this if: Need heat exposure while protecting training quality.

5 min easy with normal cooling → 35 min 120–155 W in sustained heat with some airflow → 5 min easy cooled. Thermal stimulus depends on actual conditions; not watts or sweating alone.

Heat

Heat Familiarization · 45 min

Do this if: New to heat or returning after time off.

5 min easy normally cooled → 25 min 120–155 W in manageable warmth with some airflow → 15 min normally cooled easy. 45:00 total.

Saturday

One gravel / durability ride · fuel it
Workout

Technical Durability · 2:30–3:00

Do this if: Handling skill deteriorates as you fatigue.

First 90 min aerobic → remaining ride on rough/technical gravel with repeated braking, cadence changes, low-speed torque and accelerations. Keep challenging terrain within your handling limits.

Workout

Late Stochastic · 3:00

Do this if: Need repeatable surges after accumulated fatigue.

2 h mainly aerobic → 3×15 min 185–205 W containing 4×20–45 sec 300–340 W each → 5 min easy between blocks → 5 min easy finish. 3:00 total.

Workout

Long Aerobic · 3:15–3:45

Do this if: Long-distance fatigue resistance is the priority.

Mostly 150–180 W on mixed terrain. Practice continuous fueling, pacing and efficient technique; don’t invent hard intervals. Keep terrain safe late in the ride.

Workout

Race Simulation · 4:00

Do this if: Need full gravel + fueling rehearsal; following day can be easy.

45 min controlled → 2:30 variable gravel terrain/power (surges, braking, reacceleration, imperfect recovery) → 30 min 185–205 W with 4×30 sec hard → 15 min easy. 4:00 total. Rehearse fueling, equipment and post-ride recovery.

Workout

Easy Gravel · 90 min

Do this if: Deloading or short on time.

Mostly 145–170 W. Flat/comfortable terrain and no planned surges.

Sunday

Day-2 durability / heat / rest · one option
Workout

Easy Day 2 · 60 min

Do this if: Recover while keeping legs moving.

145–165 W.

Workout

Day 2 Aerobic · 1:30

Do this if: Practicing Day-2 durability with manageable stress.

15 min easy → 65 min 155–175 W → 10 min easy. 1:30 total. Watch power/RPE drift relative to Saturday.

Workout

Day 2 Tempo · 1:45

Do this if: Saturday was controlled and you can pedal strongly today.

25 min aerobic → 2×20 min 180–195 W, 10 min easy between → 30 min aerobic finish. 1:45 total. Fuel normally; shorten if power/RPE collapses.

Workout

Day 2 Technical · 1:45

Do this if: Practice handling terrain while tired.

45 min aerobic → 45 min technical gravel with braking/reacceleration and cadence shifts → 15 min easy finish.

Heat

Heat Endurance · 60 min

Do this if: Want a substantial heat exposure after an easier Saturday.

5 min normally cooled → 50 min easy 120–155 W in genuinely warm conditions with some airflow → 5 min cool. 60:00 total. Reduce watts as needed; no deliberate dehydration.

Heat

Heat Endurance · 75 min

Do this if: Can sustain heat safely without compromising recovery.

5 min normally cooled → 60 min easy 120–155 W in sustained warmth with some airflow → 10 min cool. 75:00 total. Use only when 60-min heat is well tolerated.

Workout

Off

Do this if: Saturday was very demanding.

Full rest.
